# Professional Writing

Polish a text for a mixed audience, often non-native English readers. Return a revised text and the reason for each change.

## Workflow

1. Ask only for what you cannot infer: audience (nationalities, roles), channel (slide, email, chat), and whether the author is present when people read it.
2. Rewrite the text.
3. Give the reasons, grouped as **Content**, **Tone and culture**, **Language**. One line per change.
4. List facts to verify (numbers, client names, spelling of company and product names).
5. Keep what works. Do not rewrite sentences that are correct and clear.

## Rules

**Content**

- Cut jokes about safety, incidents, money, health, politics, religion, or a former client. Humor stays light and about the author.
- Check confidentiality. A client name, a figure or an incident in a public text needs the author's right to name it.
- Do not claim more than the author did. If the author assembled parts, do not write "built". Use the exact term (for example "bare-metal servers").
- Keep one personal fact. It builds trust. Add the dates if it explains an absence.
- End with a statement about the future, not a request for a reply, when the author cannot get answers (slide, announcement).

**Tone and culture**

- Show humility next to achievements: add "learn from you" once. Do not lecture.
- Germany: concrete facts, no exaggeration, no filler. Albania: personal warmth, family, respect for the new person in the role. A short personal line plus factual competence serves both.
- Use inclusive greetings ("Hi everyone"). Do not claim a meeting that has not happened ("nice to meet you" when absent).
- Do not guess a language phrase of a culture. Offer it only if a native speaker reviews it.

**Language**

- Remove idioms, slang, sarcasm markers, chat abbreviations, and wordplay. Machine translation and non-native readers fail on them.
- Short sentences, active voice, one idea per sentence.
- Vary the sentence starts. Many sentences with "I" in a row sound self-centered. Keep one or two.
- Fix collocations and grammar errors (for example "logistics startup", "get married").
- Prose rules of the user (no em dashes, ASD-STE100) apply.

## Output format

1. The revised text in a quote block.
2. "What I changed and why", grouped as above.
3. "Please check" list.
4. One line that offers a German or other-language version.
